# Territory research – control of map regions c. 1000–1721

Working notes for the map layer (`regions` ids in `docs/CONTENT-BRIEF.md`).
Status values: **core** · **loose-tributary** · **pledged** · **occupied** · **disputed**.

**Source policy.** URLs in the key at the end were opened (WebFetch) or seen in search results during
the research session (2026-09-23). A source marked *(book)* is a standard scholarly work cited from
knowledge and **not** checked page-by-page in this session – treat such dates as needing a second check
before they appear in event texts. **[?]** marks points that remain uncertain. Nationalencyklopedin
history articles (paywalled) and Britannica (HTTP 403) could not be opened; Wikipedia was used only
for orientation and is never cited.

Books referred to:
Harrison = Dick Harrison, *Sveriges historia 600–1350* (Norstedts 2009) ·
Norstedts = *Norstedts Sveriges historia* 1350–1600 / 1600–1721 (Norstedts 2009–10) ·

Hansen & Olsen = L. I. Hansen & B. Olsen, *Hunters in Transition: An Outline of Early Sámi History* (Brill 2014) ·
